## Operator rulings 2026-10-20 (binding; the design gate adjudicates shape inside them and never re-opens them)

- **Door A = reading (ii).** `RunSchedule` gains a separate required `diagnostic_samples` field; `importance_samples` stays the development curve ending in 64 (the registered headline `eval/*_k64`), and 256 is the fixed diagnostic subset carried by the new field. This is a one-way door: the profile manifest schema version is bumped (the current literal `h-run-profile-v1` in `profile.py` becomes its successor; no omitted field is ever filled from a runtime default, so every manifest and fixture that constructs a `RunSchedule` carries the field explicitly). The evaluation record and the metric extractor emit the diagnostic rows under their own k (256) exactly as the curve rows are keyed. The implementer proposes the field's validation (positive integer, strictly greater than `importance_samples[-1]`), its envelope role at `evaluation_records.py:120` (the weight matrix now spans the diagnostic count), and its measured cost; the design gate rules the shape.
- **Door B = in scope, as recommended.** The per-checkpoint reseed at `execution.py:331` becomes a one-line step-keyed seed derivation from `self.profile.seed` and `self.step` with a fixed, documented mixing; pinned by a distinct-draw test (two different steps draw different `log_weights` on the same held-out batch) and a same-step-reproduces test. No RNG plumbing through `scoring.py`; if the one-line form is impossible, return DECISION rather than widen.
- **Consequence of A (ii) for the frozen fixture.** `tests/test_h_probe_launch.py:345` carries a frozen tiny manifest payload; under the schema bump it must be RE-FROZEN deliberately (the lane edits it, records the old and new payload digests in the DECISION, and keeps the test asserting the new frozen bytes). It is therefore an Allowed file for this lane, and that re-freeze is the only reason.
