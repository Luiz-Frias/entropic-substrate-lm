## Why

A block that warms a sharing pair's members independently is excluded as a defect (skeleton section 7), and nothing can hand a warmed codec today: `SentenceExecution.__init__` always builds a fresh model at warm-up step 0 (ladder/run/execution.py:66-108); `resume()` needs a fresh engine (:145-162); the first seal must be codec_warmup step 1 (ladder/run/seals.py:232-245); every node checks `run_identity` (seals.py:332) and `checkpoint_binding` carries `run_identity` and the spine spec with its carrier (ladder/run/checkpoint.py:134-165). Warm-up is shareable within (MM, MS) and (SM, SS) by construction: training.py:46-56 trains only codec roles, `codec_warmup_loss` calls only `codec.reconstruct`, the law reads `law.mode` and never `spine.carrier`, construction order is codec, spine, head after `torch.manual_seed`, and prediction builds a fresh optimizer and sampler at `begin_prediction` (execution.py:138-143). The hand-off payload is the codec weights, the warm-up AdamW state and the RNG state.

## Design (brief-ready)

New member ladder/run/codec_handoff.py, reads sealed bytes only: (1) `read_seal` the donor's warm-up-final seal; (2) pair rule: manifests equal except `name` and `spine.carrier`, same `law.mode`, carriers differ (`h_preservation` may import this, never the reverse; `codec_handoff` never imports `h_preservation`, keeping the member identity closed); (3) build the recipient engine, load the donor checkpoint (restores the donor's RNG); (4) guard that every non-codec tensor equals the recipient's fresh construction, else `construction_divergence`; (5) copy codec tensors and optimizer state, set the sampler cursor and step to `warmup_steps`, draw no random numbers; (6) write the hand-off record `h-codec-handoff-v1` (donor manifest and identity, donor seal reference and checkpoint sha256, sha256 of codec tensors, optimizer and RNG state, `parent: null`), `save_checkpoint(measurement = record digest)`, the hand-off seal `h-codec-handoff-seal-v1` (`run_identity` = the recipient's, phase codec_warmup, step warmup_steps, checkpoint = the recipient's own, journal = the record), and a progress receipt. Per-cell walks check the hand-off seal and return `SealedStep(codec_warmup, warmup_steps)`; the existing warm-up-to-prediction rule (seals.py:236-241) then admits prediction step 1 unchanged. Pair check (pod and host): walk the donor's chain, require its warm-up-final seal digest equal the cited one, load both checkpoints and require equal codec, optimizer and RNG state. `CodecHandoffDefect` closed set: `independent_warmup` (structural: the recipient's chain starts with an ordinary step-1 seal; maps to the block-exclusion vocabulary owned by h1-p0, `H1ExclusionReason` in ladder/register/h_preservation.py, merged in #414), `foreign_donor`, `pair_predicate`, `chained_handoff`, `construction_divergence`, `payload_mismatch`. `h_aggregate.load_h_run` admits a recipient (no warm-up rows) only with hand-off evidence (ladder/analyze/h_aggregate.py:436; the `warmup_steps=` override must not admit a truncated stream); the recipient's exposure follows h1-p0's exposure rule (`h1_exposure_bytes`), and the acceptance test feeds a hand-off recipient `HRun` through L3's adapter (h_aggregate.py:430-440 admission; `observe_h1_blocks` in ladder/analyze/h1_observations.py, merged in #416).
